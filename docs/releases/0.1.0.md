# Quill 0.1.0

Offline Android reading, released 2026-11-07. App version **0.1.0**, Android build **2**.

## APK

Download [**Quill-0.1.0.apk**](https://github.com/sujay-patni/quill/releases/download/v0.1.0/Quill-0.1.0.apk)
and its [SHA-256 checksum](https://github.com/sujay-patni/quill/releases/download/v0.1.0/Quill-0.1.0.apk.sha256). The optimized universal APK supports
ARM64, ARMv7 and x86_64 on Android 8.0/API 26 or newer. Size: 70,500,557 bytes (~70.5 MB).
Signed with the existing development certificate for compatible sideload updates.

SHA-256:

```text
10342408f386aeccd15945579810dad0fe3f6ea382ab0a1430f46accea709557
```

Install over the existing Quill app to retain its library and reading data. Original books stay
in their selected folders; this APK contains no personal books or reading records.

## Included

- Offline EPUB, PDF and CBZ reading; Android folder picker access and extracted covers.
- Cover-only bookcase with continuous full-width shelves, dark oak bars and a compact-list option.
  Searchable series/author/tag/folder selection, shelf filters and sorting.
- Textured paper, day/night themes, typography/layout controls and a 300 ms page curl spanning
  the app's full paper frame/footer. Horizontal swipes and volume-key turns share the curl.
- Reliable slider seeking, tappable current/total position entry and a consistent five-second
  jump undo. EPUB numbers are stable book text positions; PDF/comic counts are document pages.
- Book information, favourites, 1–5 ratings, finished status, reading statistics and finish prompts.
- Custom image covers and current-EPUB-page covers, with restoration of the embedded cover.
- Moon Reader import with preview, preserved raw highlights and explicitly approximate imported
  positions until their first successful open. Source books are read in place.
- Fixes for footer/keyboard-triggered re-pagination, overlapping/silent jumps, filter overflow and
  shelf planks stopping at the side frames.


## Known issues and limits

- Large cold EPUB chapters can still take time to paginate; repeated layout-triggered pagination
  was removed. The measured large reference book reported its first position ~2.65 s after tap.
- One older saved-position fallback around installation remains unreproduced and under monitoring.
- Some EPUB author metadata displays “Last, First”.
- Typed EPUB position entry is phone-verified; PDF/CBZ typed entry has not had a separate phone check.
- Android system status/navigation icons remain fixed while app-painted paper turns.
